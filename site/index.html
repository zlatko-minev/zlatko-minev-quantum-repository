Everything here is © Zlatko K. Minev and shared under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0: read it, download it, pass it along with credit. Want a slide or figure for your own talk, course or paper? Please ask. The answer is usually yes, and it's nice to hear where the material ends up.

Some slides include figures and images by other people, credited where they appear. Those belong to their owners, so please ask the original source for them. If you spot something that's missing a credit or shouldn't be here, say so and it will be fixed.
